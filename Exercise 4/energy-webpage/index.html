SVG Elements Reference

SVG Element What It Draws
<rect> Sky background, grass field, house wall, door panel, tree trunk
<circle> Sun, tree foliage circles, doorknob
<line> Sun rays and the plus-shaped window frame
<polygon> Roof triangle above the house
<ellipse> Ground shadow under the tree
<text> Sky label text
<linearGradient> Gradient fills for sky, grass, leaves, and wall shading
<radialGradient> Sun glow effect
<g> Grouping tree shapes, window elements, and moving the house scene with transforms

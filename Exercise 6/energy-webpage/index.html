SVG Elements Reference

SVG Element What It Draws
<rect> Sky background, grass field, house wall, door panel, tree trunk
<circle> Sun, tree foliage circles, doorknob
<line> Sun rays and the plus-shaped window frame
<polygon> Roof triangle above the house
<ellipse> Ground shadow under the tree
<text> Sky label text
<linearGradient> Gradient fills for sky, grass, leaves, and wall shading
<radialGradient> Sun glow effect
<g> Grouping tree shapes, window elements, and moving the house scene with transforms

Energy Data Dashboard

Interactive D3.js visualizations: Scatter Plot, Donut Chart, Bar Chart & Line Chart

Scatter Plot - Star Rating vs Energy Consumption

How energy efficiency (stars) relates to annual kWh usage

Key Insight: Higher-rated TVs tend to use less energy, while lower-star models are more likely to consume more power.

Donut Chart - Energy by Screen Technology (All Sizes)

Average energy consumption per technology type

Key Insight: Compare LCD, LED and OLED average consumption to see which screen technology is most efficient overall.

Bar Chart - 55-inch TV Energy by Technology

Average energy consumption for 55-inch TVs only

Key Insight: This chart highlights the energy differences between LCD, LED and OLED at a common 55-inch screen size.

Line Chart - Electricity Spot Prices (1998-2024)

Average price per megawatt hour by year

Key Insight: The average price trend shows how electricity costs have moved over the last 25 years.
